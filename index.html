SMART FILTRATION

Less plumbing. Less infrastructure. A cleaner way to plan filtration.

Conventional sand-filter installations can require substantial plumbing, a pump room and balance tank depending on the pool design. Our smart/pipeless approach is designed to simplify the filtration setup in suitable applications.

  • Reduced plumbing requirements
  • Can reduce the need for a conventional pump room
  • Can avoid a separate balance tank in suitable designs
  • Single-phase operation options
  • Low maintenance
  • Lifetime service support
  • Water, chemical and electricity use can be kept efficient depending on system design

How we select the system

There is no single filtration system for every pool. Pool volume, turnover requirement, location, access, usage and site conditions are considered before the final configuration is proposed.

01 — Pool size

We consider dimensions and water volume.

02 — Site

Access, available space and civil conditions matter.

03 — Usage

Residential, farmhouse, resort or commercial use changes the requirement.

04 — Low Maintenance

Designed for simpler operation, supported by lifetime service.

Home / Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked questions

A few of the questions customers usually have before discussing a pool project.

Do ready-made pools need conventional RCC construction?GFRP/prefabricated pools can reduce or avoid conventional RCC pool construction in suitable installations. Site and structural requirements are assessed for each project.
Can you make a pool in any shape and size?Custom dimensions and designs can be discussed according to the product type, site and technical requirements.
Can an old tiled or RCC pool be renovated?Yes. We undertake pool renovation, leak repair and membrane-based solutions after inspecting the existing pool.
Can you handle terrace, podium or elevated pool projects?Such projects can be assessed. Structural support, access, load and site conditions must be evaluated before proposing a solution.
How long does a ready-made pool take?Typical delivery can be around 10–15 days after order for applicable ready-made pools, followed by installation depending on site readiness and civil-work cooperation.
How do I get a quotation?Send your location, approximate dimensions, site photographs and intended use by WhatsApp or phone. We can then discuss the suitable solution.
